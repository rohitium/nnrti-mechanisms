# Captions for Figures 3 and 4

Written against the figures as they stand now (18 genotypes; Figure 3 has 7
metrics, Figure 4 has 5). Both drop the Tyr188 interplanar angle, which no
genotype resolves — if you reinstate it, the sentence about dropped metrics
needs to change with it.

---

**Figure 3. Changes in RT–DOR contacts across the genotype panel, referenced
to wild type.** Each cell gives the shift of a structural metric from its
wild-type value, in that metric's own units, above the standard error of the
shift. Errors combine the genotype's and wild type's standard errors over
three independent 100 ns replicates in quadrature, since wild type is itself a
three-replicate mean. Colour is scaled within each column, so the darkest cell
in a column is the largest shift for that metric; it carries no meaning across
columns, which are in ångström, degrees and counts. Genotypes are grouped by
the resistance category of Table 1 and ordered by fold reduction in doravirine
susceptibility within each group. Metrics for which no genotype's 95%
confidence interval excluded zero are not shown; on this criterion the Tyr188 /
chlorocyanophenyl interplanar angle was removed. Of the 126 genotype–metric
comparisons plotted, 21 have a 95% interval excluding zero. Per-comparison
effect sizes, confidence intervals and p values for every metric, including
those not plotted, are in Supplementary Table X.

---

**Figure 4. Mutation-induced changes in RT's own conformation, as the
difference between the bound and unliganded states, referenced to wild type.**
Each cell gives (holo − apo) for a genotype minus (holo − apo) for wild type:
the extent to which binding doravirine reshapes the enzyme, and how far that
reshaping differs from wild type's. A value near zero means the mutation moves
the bound and unliganded states by the same amount — concordant remodelling,
which cancels in a thermodynamic cycle and is therefore invisible to ΔΔGbind
by construction. Errors propagate the standard errors of all four
three-replicate means in quadrature. Colour, grouping and ordering are as in
Figure 3. Metrics for which no genotype's 95% confidence interval excluded zero
are not shown; on this criterion the Cα RMSF of the YMDD motif, the p66 thumb
and the p66 connection subdomains, and the β9–β10 strand content were all
removed, and we report these as unresolved at three replicates rather than as
unchanged. Of the 90 genotype–metric comparisons plotted, 11 have a 95%
interval excluding zero.

---

## Two things to decide

**The dropped-metric sentence in Figure 4 is doing real work.** The four
metrics removed are the subdomain and catalytic-motif fluctuations — precisely
the slow, loop-rich remodelling Reviewer 1 asks about for the P225 and M230
genotypes. Saying so in the caption is the honest answer to that part of the
comment, but it is also a concession, and it may read better in the Results
text with only a pointer in the caption. Your call.

**The 95% criterion is not the obvious one.** For Figure 3 a shift must exceed
2.78 standard errors, not 2; for Figure 4, 2.31. The difference is the degrees
of freedom — two groups of three replicates against four. If a reader checks
the figure with a mental factor of 2 they will think more cells are resolved
than are. A half-sentence in Methods stating the criterion would prevent that.
