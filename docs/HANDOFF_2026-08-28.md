## 1. THE CRITICAL FINDING — do not score MM/GBSA on OpenCL

**Single-precision OpenCL silently produces catastrophic MM/GBSA energies on
frames containing severe atomic overlaps.** Two of 32 tested overlap frames blew
up:

| run | frame | OpenCL | CPU (double) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Y188L rep1 | 175 | **+289,229,277** | −45.26 |
| Y318F rep2 | 138 | **+12,050** | −65.89 |

Mechanism: frame 138 has a hydrogen–hydrogen contact at **0.216 Å**
(LYS100:HG3 ↔ ligand H1x). The r⁻¹² gradient (~10¹³) overflows single precision,
`LocalEnergyMinimizer` aborts before its first step, and the energy is returned
unminimised. The tell is that the value is **identical to the cent at 100, 500,
2000 and 10000 iterations** — the minimiser is not running at all. Heavy-atom
geometry in that frame is fine (1.97 Å min) and ligand bond lengths are normal;
only the hydrogen placement is broken, consistent with the DCD-writing artifact
described in `results/analysis/binding_energy/MMGBSA_METHOD_AND_RECOMPUTE.md`.

Even excluding blow-ups, OpenCL disagrees with CPU by up to **3.67 kcal/mol** on
overlap frames (mean 0.37), versus 0.059 kcal/mol on clean frames.

**Why the earlier platform validation missed it:** it compared CPU and OpenCL on
six runs drawn from the *contact-screened* frame set, so it contained no
pathological frames — precisely the population that later stopped being
excluded. `platform_validation_cpu_vs_opencl.csv` is therefore valid only for the
screened protocol. Any future platform check must include overlap frames.

**RESOLVED 2026-08-28 22:51** — canonical panel relaunched on CPU at **2000
minimisation iterations** (was 100), no frame screening, 12 workers, output
`.checkpoint_mmgbsa_cpu2000_2026-08-29.csv`. ~20-25 h.

The iteration sweep (`minimisation_convergence_cpu.txt`) showed the 100-iteration
cap was the larger problem: overlap frames were not converged, and the apparent
"7 kcal/mol residual" was mostly unfinished minimisation, not a property of

| iters | overlap | clean | gap |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 | -50.83 | -63.44 | **12.6** |

## 2. MM/GBSA protocol — where it landed and why

Three protocols now exist. Comparison:
`results/analysis/binding_energy/protocol_comparison_three_way.csv`.

| protocol | frames | filtering | mean SEM | max | >1.0 |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| terminal-20 (published) | last 20 | contact screen | 1.07 | 2.55 | 9/18 |
| even-100 screened | 100 even | contact screen | **0.59** | 1.31 | 3/18 |
| even-100 unfiltered | 100 even | none | pending CPU rerun | | |

**Two independent changes, do not conflate them.**

**(a) Sampling window — settled, keep.** The terminal-20 protocol scored the
final ~10 ns of each 100 ns run. A variance decomposition shows only a median
**4%** of between-replicate variance is 20-frame noise, so more terminal frames
would not help. A five-window scan across four systems shows the replicate spread
collapses when the whole post-equilibration region is sampled (G190E 4.41 →
1.10, Y188L 3.80 → 0.64 kcal/mol): adjacent terminal frames are strongly
autocorrelated, so each replicate mean landed on whatever excursion its run ended
in. Evidence: `window_scan_2026-08-28.csv`.

Gated on the ligand staying in one binding mode
(`screen_mmgbsa_pose_stability.py`): DOR RMSD to reference 1.27 Å mean, spread
about each run's own mean 0.14 Å median. One of 60 runs flagged (G190S rep 3,
3.17 Å max but 0.22 Å self-spread — displaced yet stable).

**(b) Contact screen retired — Rohit's decision, has a cost.** Frames are no
longer discarded; overlaps are handled by minimisation. Rationale: the artifact
guarded against is ~3300 kcal/mol, so a ~7 kcal/mol residual after minimisation
is immaterial. Verified the minimiser does repair contacts (0.92 Å → 3.01 Å at
100 iterations, 0.44 Å geometry drift). Evidence:
`contact_screen_retirement_test.csv`, `minimisation_drift_test.csv`.

The cost is real and concentrated in heavily-flagged runs. Y188L is the clean
example:

| rep | ΔG | per-frame σ | frames the screen flagged |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | −57.55 | 4.65 | **122/180 (68%)** |
| 2 | −61.01 | 2.04 | 0 |
| 3 | −62.57 | 2.04 | 10 (6%) |

Y188L SEM goes 0.55 (screened) → ~1.48 (unfiltered), because rep1 — two thirds
of whose frames were being removed — now pulls away from its siblings. Flagged
frames sit ~2.0 Å from the clean mean DOR pose versus ~0.7 Å for clean frames,
so including them genuinely widens the sampled ensemble.

**Open question for the author:** are those frames sampling you want in the
average, or corruption you want out? The evidence is genuinely two-sided —
the pre-minimisation energies are thermodynamically unreachable at 300 K
(suggesting artifact), but post-minimisation they are physical structures in a
displaced pose (suggesting sampling). This is a scientific call, not a technical
one. It was decided in favour of keeping them; revisit only with new evidence.

## 4. Getting every SEM below 0.5 kcal/mol

Costed 2026-08-28. `SEM = σ/√n`, so `n = 4σ²` — the payoff from reducing σ is
quadratic, from adding replicates only linear. **Reduce σ first.**

### MM/GBSA — 67 extra replicates (~2,500 GPU-h)
Eight of 18 genotypes already qualify. Worst offenders and required n:

| genotype | σ | SEM now | n needed | extra |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| L100I+K103N | 2.26 | 1.31 | 21 | 18 |
| V106M | 2.19 | 1.26 | 20 | 17 |
| G190S | 1.85 | 1.07 | 14 | 11 |
| V106I | 1.69 | 0.97 | 12 | 9 |
| V106A+F227L | 1.31 | 0.76 | 7 | 4 |
| G190E / K103N+M230L / G190A / Y188L / A98G+F227C | | | | 1–3 each |

Checked: **not** outlier-driven. L100I+K103N's replicates are +2.51 / −1.93 /
+1.07 — a genuine 4.4 kcal/mol spread; dropping any one does not rescue it. So
replicates are the only lever here. New replicates come from
`src/structure_prep/preparation.py --replicates N`, which generates rep_04+
idempotently with fresh jitter seeds and leaves reps 1–3 untouched.

### FEP — 138 extra leg-replicates, and the naive route is impossible
Solved over the shared-leg structure (`wt_to_K103N` feeds four genotypes):

| leg | σ_ΔΔG | n needed | extra |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| wt_to_G190E | 3.06 | **38** | 35 |
| V106I_to_V106I_F227C | 1.56 | 20 | 17 |
| V106A_to_V106A_P225H | 1.74 | 16 | 13 |
| wt_to_Y181C | 1.84 | 14 | 11 |

≈1,100–1,650 GPU array elements. **The blocker is seeding, not switching:** each

**Therefore: σ-reduction first.** The 20 ns equilibration campaign now running is
exactly this test. If it takes G190E's σ from 3.06 to ~1.4, the requirement falls
from n=38 to n=8 — the difference between impossible and a weekend.

### Shared bottleneck
Extra MD replicates serve **both** campaigns: the same rep_04+ runs feed MM/GBSA
and seed FEP legs. Plan them once. Apo runs are needed for FEP seeding but not
for MM/GBSA.
