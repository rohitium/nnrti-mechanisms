# Literature support for the Discussion

Updated 4 November 2026. The changes were applied to the author's latest saved `submission-ready/Manuscript-Marked.docx`; the clean manuscript was not overwritten.

Eight papers were added to Zotero. Existing library records were reused for Ghosh et al., Pelemans et al., Geitmann et al., Hecksel et al., and Rhee et al. Word's Zotero Refresh regenerated citation numbering and the bibliography.

| Discussion claim | Primary evidence | Scope and limits |

| Alchemical calculations can miss slowly interconverting conformations and depend on starting structures | Wang, Berne and Friesner (2012), [On achieving high accuracy and reliability in the calculation of relative protein–ligand binding affinities](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1114017109) | Direct computational tests of protein and ligand reorganization, with enhanced sampling improving convergence. General methodological evidence, not a measurement of error in this study. |
| Replicate agreement is not proof of exhaustive sampling | Knapp, Ospina and Deane (2018), [Avoiding False Positive Conclusions in Molecular Simulation: The Importance of Replicas](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00391) | Large ensembles show sensitivity to replica selection and simulation duration. Complements the existing nonequilibrium free-energy citation. |
| The primer grip couples substrate placement to polymerase and RNase H functions | Ghosh et al. (1996), [Alterations to the primer grip of p66 HIV-1 reverse transcriptase and their consequences for template-primer utilization](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi952773j) | Mutagenesis provides direct functional evidence. Does not demonstrate the proposed DOR mechanism for P225H or K103N+M230L. |
| Productive RT binding orientations depend on substrates and are altered by small molecules | Abbondanzieri et al. (2008), [Dynamic binding orientations direct activity of HIV reverse transcriptase](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06941) | Single-molecule experiments show opposite effects of cognate nucleotides and NVP on orientation dynamics. Supports substrate-dependent inhibition, not a DOR mutation-specific mechanism. |
| P225H can arise with V106A and contribute additional NNRTI resistance | Pelemans et al. (1997), [Characteristics of the Pro225His mutation …](https://doi.org/10.1128/JVI.71.11.8195-8203.1997) | Selection and biochemical evidence involving quinoxaline and other older NNRTIs. No DOR-specific conclusion is assigned to this work. |
| P225H, K103N and L100I can affect inhibitor kinetics | Geitmann, Unge and Danielson (2006), [Interaction kinetic characterization of HIV-1 reverse transcriptase non-nucleoside inhibitor resistance](https://doi.org/10.1021/jm0504050) | Kinetic effects differ among inhibitors. The paper reports faster entry and exit for K103N/L100I, qualifying the earlier blanket claim that K103N slows entry. |
| F227C can communicate structurally with the nucleotide-binding region | Hecksel et al. (2026), [Allosteric Crosstalk between Inhibitor Binding Sites Drives Enhanced Islatravir Susceptibility in F227C HIV-1 Reverse Transcriptase](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsinfecdis.6c00431) | Structural precedent involving ISL susceptibility. Does not establish why A98G+F227C resists DOR. |
| Enzyme stability and proteolysis can affect viral phenotype beyond purified-enzyme activity | Dunn et al. (2015), [Mutations in human immunodeficiency virus type 1 reverse transcriptase that make it sensitive to degradation by the viral protease in virions are selected against in patients](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.virol.2015.05.020) | Independent experimental support for the general omitted process. The existing Huang et al. study remains the direct source for G190E/G190S virion-associated RT phenotypes. |
| DOR has substrate-dependent effects on RNase H that distal mutations can alter | Álvarez et al. (2021), [Analysis and Molecular Determinants of HIV RNase H Cleavage Specificity at the PPT/U3 Junction](https://doi.org/10.3390/v13010131) | DOR enhances PPT cleavage in biochemical assays, and N348I/T369I attenuates this effect. These substitutions are outside the panel; this is not proof of cellular DOR resistance or of an M230L mechanism. |
| Initiation and elongation involve distinct substrate dynamics | Liu et al. (2010), [Initiation complex dynamics direct the transitions between distinct phases of early HIV reverse transcription](https://doi.org/10.1038/nsmb.1937) | Single-molecule evidence complements the existing Ha et al. initiation-complex structures. |
| QM/MM can investigate HIV RT catalytic chemistry | Wang et al. (2020), [Exploring the Reaction Mechanism of HIV Reverse Transcriptase with a Nucleotide Substrate](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.0c02632) | Direct RT application combining QM/MM, a string method and Milestoning. Replaces the less directly relevant HIV protease application at this citation location. |
| More genotype-resolved public DOR phenotype data are needed | Rhee et al. (2023), [Potential role of doravirine for the treatment of HIV-1-infected persons with transmitted drug resistance](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12981-023-00503-5) | Identifies gaps in available mutation-specific DOR susceptibility measurements. The manuscript's September 2026 HIVDB counts remain its own dated database inventory. |
| Site-directed mutants and clinical genetic backgrounds can yield different susceptibility distributions | Giammarino et al. (2024), [Prevalence and Phenotypic Susceptibility to Doravirine of the HIV-1 Reverse Transcriptase V106I Polymorphism in B and Non-B Subtypes](https://doi.org/10.1093/infdis/jiae010) | V106I remained below the DOR cutoff in site-directed mutants, while some clinical recombinant viruses exceeded it. Supports reporting sequence context; it does not isolate every contributing background substitution. |

## Interpretations that remain hypotheses

The cited literature supports the biological plausibility of omitted processes. It does not demonstrate that M230L-mediated reduction in RNase H activity causes DOR resistance, that P225H changes productive-state populations in the simulated genotypes, or that the specific apo rotamer changes observed here cause resistance. Statements based on this study's own trajectories should retain their Table/Figure references and their existing qualifications rather than acquire citations that imply independent validation.
