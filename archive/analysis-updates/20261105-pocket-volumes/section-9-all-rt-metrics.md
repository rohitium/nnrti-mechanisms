Three 100 ns apo replicates were run for WT and all nineteen simulated mutant genotypes, including the F227C alchemical intermediate. Supplementary Table 4 reports the per-replicate structural observables in both states and the WT-referenced bound–unliganded differences. Supplementary Figure 6 shows the replicate estimates for all ten RT conformational metrics used in Figure 5: NNIBP volume, Tyr181 and Tyr188 χ1, Cα RMSF in six regions, and β9–β10 strand content. WT and all eighteen mutant genotypes other than the standalone F227C alchemical intermediate are shown. These measurements describe the sampled RT conformations; they do not quantify the free energy required to form the pocket.
