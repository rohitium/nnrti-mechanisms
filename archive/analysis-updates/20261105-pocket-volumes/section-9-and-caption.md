9. Conformation of the unliganded binding pocket

Three 100 ns apo replicates were run for WT and all nineteen simulated mutant genotypes, including the F227C alchemical intermediate. Supplementary Table 4 reports pocket volumes in both states, the per-replicate values and the WT-referenced bound–unliganded differences alongside the other structural observables. Supplementary Figure 6 shows the per-frame pocket-volume distributions for WT and all eighteen other mutant genotypes; the standalone F227C alchemical intermediate is omitted from the figure. The mean unliganded pocket volume is smaller than the bound-state mean in every genotype. These measurements describe pocket geometry; they do not quantify the free energy required to form the pocket.
