The reviewer asks whether a mutation remodels the unliganded enzyme as well as the bound one, and whether such remodeling would therefore be poorly reflected in ΔΔGbind. This is a measurement rather than a hypothesis, because the thermodynamic cycle introduces each mutation twice, once in each state. A perturbation of equal size in both states cancels, and the calculated binding free energy cannot register it; only a perturbation that differs between the two states survives the cycle. Writing d(apo) for the shift from wild type in the unliganded state and d(holo) for the shift from wild type in the bound state, a genotype with d(apo) = d(holo) is invisible to ΔΔGbind however large either quantity is on its own.

Across the ten metrics and eighteen clinical genotypes, 167 of the 178 available comparisons are concordant: the mutation moves the unliganded and the bound ensemble by the same amount within error. The enzyme is remodelled, and for the great majority of these observables the cycle is blind to it. The eleven exceptions are the pocket volume of G190E (+71 ± 26 Å³), G190S (+65 ± 27 Å³) and V106A+P225H (−48 ± 18 Å³); the Tyr181 χ1 of A98G+F227C (−76 ± 11°), G190S (−82 ± 28°) and V106I (−16 ± 5°); the Tyr188 χ1 of Y318F (+95 ± 32°); the pocket-lining Cα RMSF of A98G+F227C (+0.11 ± 0.04 Å) and V106I (+0.07 ± 0.03 Å); and the primer-grip Cα RMSF of K103N+M230L (−0.16 ± 0.06 Å) and V106I (+0.14 ± 0.06 Å). Each is the induced-fit difference d(holo) − d(apo), with the error propagating the standard errors of all four three-replicate means in quadrature, and each is a resolved cell of Figure 5.

The two genotypes the reviewer raises behave differently from one another. G190E has the largest bound pocket in the panel, 56 Å³ above wild type, but its unliganded pocket is if anything slightly smaller than wild type's, by 15 Å³. Its pocket expansion is therefore an induced-fit effect rather than a property of the resting enzyme, and it is one of the few changes the cycle can in principle register; that it coincides with a favourable calculated ΔΔGbind means the expansion is not by itself paid for in binding energy, not that it was cancelled. Every other G190E observable, including all seven fluctuation measures, is concordant between the two states. K103N+M230L is the opposite case: its unliganded primer grip is markedly more mobile than wild type's, 0.772 ± 0.041 Å against 0.570 ± 0.043 Å, and binding doravirine suppresses that excess to 0.474 ± 0.012 Å. That genotype is concordant on six of its seven fluctuation metrics and discordant only here.

The loop-rich P225- and M230-containing genotypes do not show a distinct unliganded conformational state at this sampling. K103N+P225H and V106A+P225H are concordant with wild type on every fluctuation metric and on both pocket tyrosines, and V106A+P225H is discordant only in pocket volume, in the direction of a smaller rather than a larger bound pocket. The three genotypes do have the widest block-to-block scatter of any in the panel in the unliganded state, K103N+P225H twice wild type's in the pocket lining and V106A+P225H 1.6 times wild type's in the primer grip, which is the signature that slower conformational remodeling would leave. That scatter is not resolved: no genotype's difference in spread from wild type survives correction for the eighteen comparisons made (Levene test on the 10 ns block values, smallest Holm-adjusted p = 0.84). Three 100 ns replicates per genotype constrain where the unliganded ensemble sits well enough to support the concordance result above, and do not constrain how broad it is; resolving the breadth would require longer runs or more of them.
