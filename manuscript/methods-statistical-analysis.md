## Statistical analysis

Uncertainties on ∆∆G<sub>bind</sub> and ∆∆E<sub>Total</sub> are standard errors
of the mean across three independent replicate simulations, propagated in
quadrature where a genotype was decomposed into sequential single-residue legs.
For the MM/GBSA components, each mutant replicate was referenced to the mean of
the three wild-type replicates; the uncertainty of that reference is a common
offset shared by every genotype and is therefore excluded from the per-genotype
standard error, which is what determines comparisons *between* genotypes.

Associations between computed energies and phenotype were assessed by ordinary
least-squares regression against log<sub>10</sub>-transformed fold-change in
susceptibility, reported as the coefficient of determination R² with the
two-sided p-value for a non-zero slope (`scipy.stats.linregress`). Fold-change
was log-transformed because susceptibility is measured as a ratio and its
untransformed distribution spans two orders of magnitude; the logarithm is also
the scale on which a change in binding free energy is expected to be linear,
since ∆∆G = −RT ln(fold) under a pure binding model. Correlation between the two
computed quantities, ∆∆G<sub>bind</sub> and ∆∆E<sub>Total</sub>, was assessed the
same way. Rank association was additionally checked with Spearman's ρ, which does
not assume linearity.

Classification performance was evaluated only for the genotypes with established
phenotypes (n = 13: 9 resistant, 4 susceptible); the five genotypes of uncertain
phenotype were excluded because they provide no reference label. A genotype was
predicted to have a strong effect on binding when its estimate lay more than one
standard error above 0.5 kcal/mol, the thermal energy at 300 K — the same
criterion used throughout the Results. Performance is reported as sensitivity,
specificity and the Matthews correlation coefficient (MCC). MCC is preferred to
accuracy here because the panel is imbalanced 9:4, so a classifier that labelled
every genotype resistant would score 0.69 accuracy while carrying no
information; MCC is high only when all four cells of the confusion matrix are
favourable. Significance of the 2 × 2 association was assessed by Fisher's exact
test (one-sided).

Because the 0.5 kcal/mol criterion is a physical constant rather than a cutoff
fitted to these data, threshold-free performance was also assessed by the area
under the receiver operating characteristic curve (ROC AUC), computed from the
raw energies via the Mann-Whitney U statistic with ties counted as one half. The
AUC confidence interval was obtained by bootstrap resampling of genotypes
(10,000 resamples, percentile interval), and its p-value by exact permutation
over all 715 possible labelings of 9 resistant among 13 genotypes, which is
enumerable at this sample size and so requires no distributional assumption.

All tests are two-sided unless stated. Given the panel size, these statistics are
reported as descriptive effect sizes with their intervals rather than as
hypothesis tests; no correction for multiple comparisons was applied, and none of
the classification statistics reaches conventional significance at n = 13.

## Suggested Results sentence

> Restricted to the 13 genotypes with established phenotypes, ∆∆G<sub>bind</sub>
> separated resistant from susceptible genotypes with a sensitivity of 0.78
> (7 of 9), a specificity of 0.75 (3 of 4) and a Matthews correlation
> coefficient of 0.50, corresponding to a threshold-free ROC AUC of 0.78
> (95% CI 0.43–1.00). The corresponding MM/GBSA classification detected
> resistance equally often (sensitivity 0.78) but misclassified three of the
> four susceptible mutations, giving a specificity of 0.25, an MCC of 0.03 and an
> AUC of 0.61 (95% CI 0.25–0.93). Neither difference reaches conventional
> significance at this panel size (Fisher exact p = 0.119 and 0.706; AUC
> permutation p = 0.074 and 0.302).

Misclassifications worth naming in the text: FEP misses A98G+F227C and
K103N+M230L (both false negatives) and over-calls V106I; MM/GBSA misses
V106I+F227C and K103N+M230L and over-calls V106I, Y181C and G190A.
